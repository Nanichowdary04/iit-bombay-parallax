The institute’s motto means “knowledge is the supreme goal”. For close to seven decades, IIT Bombay has turned that line into engineers, scientists, designers and founders.

Since 1958

The second IIT. Built with the world’s help.

IIT Bombay was set up in 1958 with assistance from UNESCO and funds from the Soviet Union, on land in Powai given by the then Bombay State Government. Its first batch had 100 students.

Code & startups

Ideas that ship.

Late-night hackathons, coding clubs and SINE, the campus incubator set up in 2004 that has backed early-stage startups for two decades.

02

Engineering teams

Steel, sparks & student teams.

Student teams design and build rovers, race cars, underwater robots and combat bots, then test them against the best in the country.

  • Mars Rover
  • IIT Bombay Racing
  • AUV
  • Robowars
03

Space

A satellite built by students.

On 26 September 2016 a PSLV rocket carried Pratham, a 10 kg satellite designed by IIT Bombay students to measure the electrons in Earth’s ionosphere.

  • Pratham
  • Aerospace Engineering
  • Drones

Festivals

And when the sun goes down…

Diwali lights on the hostel lawns, Mood Indigo’s concerts, and Techfest, the science and technology festival students have run every year since 1998.
